I’m Sorry (Sort Of.)

I need to ask you something…

No tricks, I promise. Mostly.

Just one question

Are you mad at me?

Take your time. I’ll wait.

A real apology

I’m sorry — really.

I hope the little chase made you smile, because what follows is completely sincere. I keep replaying what I said, and I don’t like how it sounded, and I like even less how it made you feel. There isn’t a good excuse, so I won’t reach for one — it was my fault, and I’m sorry you carried that.

Mostly I’m sorry for being quiet when I should have spoken up, and for pushing away while hoping you’d stay close. You matter more to me than being right ever will.

Take your time. I’m here, and I’m listening.
